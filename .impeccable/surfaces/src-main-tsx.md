# Home directory

## Scope

- **Mode:** Operate
- **Audience:** SQU students choosing a planning task on a phone or laptop.
- **Task:** Identify the appropriate tool and enter it quickly, while seeing that its timetable data has a known snapshot date.
- **Constraints:** Bilingual LTR/RTL; static and local-first; no live-availability or institutional-endorsement claims; semantic and keyboard-accessible.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A quiet academic workspace makes the directory read like a maintained student service, refusing the old game-launcher bands and oversized type.

**OWN-WORLD:** A light neutral canvas, charcoal ink, cool-gray dividers, and one calm deep-blue action/focus color. System UI type, slim rules, native-feeling controls, and no shadows, gradients, glass, or decorative cards.

**STORY:** The student immediately understands this is a collection of planning tools, scans their purpose and availability, opens the needed tool, and can verify the timetable snapshot’s freshness without mistaking it for live data.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact brand-and-language header sits above a modest page title and one explanatory sentence. Three full-width, divider-separated directory rows follow: name and purpose left, status and directional affordance right. The snapshot record sits quietly below the list.

**FORM:** Quiet Academic Workspace, a user-pinned replacement direction for the former Night Noticeboard. Direction seed: `e4e8479a`; the user-pinned direction supersedes its assigned candidate.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
